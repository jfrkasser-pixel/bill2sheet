These apply to new scans and translations. Nothing here leaves your device except the OCR text used for translation, as noted earlier.

With no key, AI stays off and nothing is sent anywhere. With a key, AI runs after each scan and you can also tap ✦ fix fields with AI. The key and the receipt photo go only to Google's Gemini API.
